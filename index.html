What this app is and isn't

Every number here is researched and dated — class profiles, fees, deadlines, visa rules, employment reports. The admit probabilities are modelled judgements, not measurements: base rates adjusted for the Indian sub-pool and for your specific profile. They assume a top-quartile application.

The advisor answers from a written knowledge base — it is not a live AI and it will not invent an answer it doesn't have. Verify every deadline on the school's own site before you rely on it. Several European programmes run rolling rounds that shift year to year.
